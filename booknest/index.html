Booknest

Your ebook library, organized and backed up

What it does

Booknest is a personal ebook library manager for EPUB, PDF, TXT, FB2, and CBZ files (with more formats planned). It helps you organize your existing ebook collection, fetch cover art and metadata, and read on your device. Your files stay on your device by default, with an optional backup to your own Google Drive or iCloud account, so you never lose your library and can free up space on your device without losing access to it.
