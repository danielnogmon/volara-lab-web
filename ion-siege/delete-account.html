Delete Your Account — Ion Siege

Last updated: September 28, 2026

Ion Siege is developed by Volara Lab. This page explains how you can delete your Ion Siege account, and what happens to your data when you do.

Option 1 — Delete it from inside the App (fastest)

  1. Open the Ion Siege app.
  2. Open Settings > Account.
  3. Tap DELETE ACCOUNT AND DATA, then confirm.

This deletes your account immediately — there is no waiting period when you delete it this way.

Option 2 — Request deletion without the App

If you no longer have access to the App, email support@volara.dev from the e-mail address linked to your account (or with any account identifier you have) and the subject line "Delete my account." We will verify the request and delete your account within 30 days, and confirm by email once it's done.

What gets deleted

  • Your account itself (sign-in record, whether guest, e-mail, Google, or Apple).
  • Your Gems balance and its transaction history.
  • Your cloud save (run progress, unlocked Towers/Modules/Talents, and similar in-game state).
  • Your Endless-mode leaderboard entries, for every Sector.

What is retained, and for how long

  • Your past purchase transaction records (product bought, store, amount, date) are kept for accounting and tax purposes, but are unlinked from your account — we can no longer tell they were yours.
  • Anonymous, aggregate analytics events already recorded before deletion (for example, "a run was started on Sector 3") are not personally identifiable and are not deleted individually, the same as for any other analytics data.
  • Progress stored locally on your device is not touched by account deletion — it stays on the device until you uninstall the App or clear its data, see Delete Your Data.

Contact

Questions about account deletion can be sent to support@volara.dev.
