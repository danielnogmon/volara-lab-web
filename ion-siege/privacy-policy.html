Privacy Policy — Ion Siege

Last updated: September 28, 2026

Ion Siege ("the App", "we", "us") is a roguelite idle tower defense game, developed by Volara Lab. This Privacy Policy explains what data the App handles, how it is stored, and what control you have over it.

1. Account — guest by default

The App creates a guest (anonymous) account for you automatically the first time you play, so your progress can sync to our server and, if you choose, to other devices. A guest account holds no personal information beyond an internal account identifier. You can optionally link an e-mail address and password, or sign in with Google or Apple, to make sure you can recover your progress on another device — see Section 2. You are never required to give us your real name.

2. Sign-in with Google or Apple (optional)

If you choose to link your guest account to Google or Apple instead of an e-mail and password, the App receives only what those providers share for sign-in purposes — typically your account identifier and, if you allow it, your e-mail address — through Firebase Authentication (operated by Google; see Google's Privacy Policy). We do not receive your Google or Apple password, and we do not post to those accounts or access any other data on them.

3. Game data stored on our server

The App is built on Firebase (operated by Google) and Cloud Functions we control. Your device never talks to our database directly — every request goes through our own server code, which stores:

  • Your Gems balance and its transaction history (a ledger of every Gems change, so the balance can't be tampered with locally).
  • Your cloud save — your run progress, unlocked Towers/Modules/Talents, and similar in-game state, so you can continue on another device.
  • Your Endless-mode leaderboard entry (best wave reached per Sector), shown publicly under a generated display name like "Commander-XXXX" — never your real name or e-mail.
  • A record of your in-app purchases (product bought, store, amount, transaction id) tied to your account, used to credit the right rewards and to prevent fraud.

We also use Firebase Analytics, and may use Firebase Crashlytics, to understand gameplay patterns and diagnose crashes — this data is tied to an anonymous installation identifier, not your name or e-mail.

4. In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Google Play or the App Store, and by RevenueCat, the subscription/purchase platform we use to manage entitlements across both stores. RevenueCat receives your purchase events from the store and forwards a confirmation to our server so we can credit your Gems or unlock what you bought — RevenueCat does not receive your library, save data, or Gems balance. We never see or store your payment card details; those are handled entirely by Google Play or the App Store.

5. Ads

The App may show optional rewarded video ads (for example, to refill Energy or double a reward) through Google AdMob. These ads are only ever shown when you choose to watch one — the App does not show interstitial or forced ads. AdMob may use an advertising identifier to serve and measure ads, governed by Google's advertising policies. Where required by local law, you'll be asked for consent before any personalized ad is shown.

6. What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data to any third party.
  • We do not ask for or store your real name, unless you choose to type one into a future in-app profile field.
  • We do not access your contacts, photos, or other apps on your device.

7. Data deletion

You can delete your account and all of the data described in Section 3 at any time from Settings > Account inside the App, or see Delete Your Account for the full steps. Uninstalling the App removes everything stored locally on your device, but does not by itself delete your account or cloud save — see Delete Your Data.

8. Children's privacy

Ion Siege is rated for players aged 13 and up and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13 (or the relevant minimum age in your jurisdiction).

9. Security

Your device never talks to our database directly — all access goes through server code we control, which enforces who can read or write what. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the App evolves. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to support@volara.dev.
