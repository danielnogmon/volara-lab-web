Delete Your Data — Ion Siege

Last updated: September 28, 2026

Ion Siege is developed by Volara Lab. Your Gems balance, cloud save, and leaderboard entries are all part of your account record on our server — the App does not currently offer a way to erase just that data while keeping the account itself signed in. If you want that data gone, use Delete Your Account, which removes your account and everything listed there in one step.

Data stored only on your device

Some data — for example, local settings and, while you're offline, your most recent local copy of your save — lives only on your device and is separate from your account. To remove that:

  • Uninstall the App. This deletes everything stored locally on your device immediately.
  • On Android, you can instead go to your device's Settings > Apps > Ion Siege > Storage > Clear Data without uninstalling, which has the same effect for local data.

Neither of these deletes your account or cloud save from our server on their own — if you're signed in, your progress simply downloads again the next time you open the App. Use Delete Your Account if you want the server-side copy gone too.
