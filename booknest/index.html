Booknest

Your ebook library manager, organized and backed up. Designed for EPUB, PDF, and various ebook formats with local-first file control.

What is Booknest?

Booknest is a personal ebook library manager for EPUB, PDF, TXT, FB2, and CBZ files (with more formats planned). It helps you organize your existing ebook collection, fetch cover art and metadata, and read comfortably on your device.

Your files, your library

Your ebook files and reading history stay on your device by default. We do not host your library, track your reading metrics, or sell your analytics to third-party databases.

Backup to your own cloud storage

Booknest offers optional backup syncing directly to your own Google Drive or iCloud account. This lets you free up space on your device without losing access to your ebooks, and keeps your collection synced across multiple devices.
